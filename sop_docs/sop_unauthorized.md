# Unauthorized Transaction Handling (SAMPLE POLICY - replace with your enterprise SOP)

## SOP-FRD-5.1 Fraud indicators
Count these indicators: transaction country differs from the customer's usual country; online or card-not-present channel; transaction between 01:00 and 05:00 local time; first-time merchant; two or more charges at the same merchant within 24 hours; amount at least 5 times the customer's average purchase.

## SOP-FRD-5.2 Immediate actions
With two or more indicators and the customer confirming the card is in their possession, block the card immediately, order a replacement card, open a fraud case, and issue provisional credit for the disputed amounts within 1 business day.

## SOP-FRD-5.3 When not to auto-credit
With fewer than two indicators, or if the customer mentions a family member, a shared device or a known merchant, do not issue credit. Route the case to manual review by the fraud team.
