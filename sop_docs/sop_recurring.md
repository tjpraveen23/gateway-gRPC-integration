# Recurring Charges After Cancellation (SAMPLE POLICY - replace with your enterprise SOP)

## SOP-REC-6.1 Proof of cancellation
A recurring charge posted after the customer's cancellation date is disputable only if the customer can state the cancellation date and provide a cancellation confirmation. Without a stated cancellation date, route to manual review.

## SOP-REC-6.2 Provisional credit
Issue provisional credit for charges posted after the cancellation date, up to 2 billing cycles. For more than 2 cycles, supervisor approval is required.

## SOP-REC-6.3 Dispute and stop payment
Raise a dispute with reason code 13.2 (Cancelled Recurring Transaction) and place a recurring-payment stop on the merchant to block future charges.
