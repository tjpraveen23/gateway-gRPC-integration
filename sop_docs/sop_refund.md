# Merchant Refund Handling (SAMPLE POLICY - replace with your enterprise SOP)

## SOP-REF-3.1 Refund posting timeline
Merchant refunds post to the card within 10 business days after the merchant issues them. If the refund was issued less than 10 business days ago, no dispute can be raised; tell the customer the expected posting date.

## SOP-REF-3.2 Overdue refunds
If more than 10 business days have passed since the merchant issued the refund and no matching REFUND appears, raise a chargeback with reason code 13.6 (Credit Not Processed). Provisional credit may be issued only after the chargeback is raised.

## SOP-REF-3.3 Required documents
For a chargeback the customer must provide the merchant's refund confirmation or return receipt. If the customer has no proof, advise them to request it from the merchant before filing.
