# Duplicate Charge Disputes (SAMPLE POLICY - replace with your enterprise SOP)

## SOP-DISP-4.1 Identifying a duplicate charge
A duplicate charge exists when two or more POSTED purchases share the same merchant and amount within 24 hours and have different authorization codes. Repeat purchases with different amounts are not duplicates.

## SOP-DISP-4.2 Provisional credit for duplicates
When a duplicate is confirmed, issue provisional credit equal to the duplicated amount immediately if the amount is 500 USD or less. Above 500 USD, supervisor approval is required before credit is issued.

## SOP-DISP-4.3 Pending authorization holds
If one entry is PENDING and shares the authorization code with a POSTED entry, it is an authorization hold and not a duplicate. Do not issue credit. Tell the customer the hold drops off automatically within 7 business days.

## SOP-DISP-4.4 Raising the dispute
If the customer asks to dispute a confirmed duplicate, raise a dispute with reason code 12.6.1 (Duplicate Processing) and attach both transaction ids. Confirm the customer has not received a refund from the merchant.
